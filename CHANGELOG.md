# Changelog

## Unreleased

### 🎉 New features

- **iOS**: Added `zoomSourceTestID`, which presents the sheet zooming out of the view with that `testID` and dismisses it back into the view, using UIKit's zoom transition on iOS 18 and later. ([#1069](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1069) by [@yasharAyari](https://github.com/yasharAyari))

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- **iOS**: VoiceOver no longer reaches the screen behind a dimmed sheet that has a `footer`. At detents below `dimmedDetentIndex`, where that screen still takes touches, VoiceOver now reaches it, footer or not. ([#1027](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1027) by [@rendis](https://github.com/rendis) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: Context menus and anything else UIKit shows over a sheet, such as a SwiftUI or `@expo/ui` `Menu`, and the sheet's system grabber are now in the accessibility hierarchy, so XCTest-based tools like Maestro, Detox, and Appium can find them. ([#1061](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1061), [#1062](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1062) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: Content that mounts as a sheet presents or resizes, such as a search field and its results, now appears in place instead of growing in from its top-left corner. ([#1051](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1051) by [@maxlapides](https://github.com/maxlapides) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: `detents` changed while a sheet presents now apply, instead of the sheet staying at the old detent while `onDidPresent` reports the new one. A sheet presented while its presenter, such as a `Modal`, is still opening now waits for it: `dismiss()` meanwhile cancels it instead of letting it appear, a second `present()` or sheet no longer hangs, and a changed `initialDetentIndex` or `initialDetentAnimated={false}` is honored. ([#1064](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1064) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: The `soft` `topScrollEdgeEffect` and `bottomScrollEdgeEffect` now end just past the `header` and `footer`, as under native bars, instead of bleeding about 30pt further into the content. With the keyboard open, a cut-off edge effect no longer shows above an absolute `footer` when the content ends at its top. ([#1066](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1066) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: `position`, detents and the sheet's max width now match `useWindowDimensions` on React Native 0.86+ below Android 15, and `position` is also correct in split-screen on Android 10 and older. With edge-to-edge off, `position` is no longer off by the system bars' size and the bottom of the sheet, including the footer, is no longer cut off. ([#1042](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1042) by [@gadcam](https://github.com/gadcam) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: Scrollable content that overflows the sheet by less than the bottom inset now scrolls, and its end clears the navigation bar instead of sitting under it. ([#1048](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1048) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: TalkBack now reaches views shown above a dimmed sheet, and a React Native `Modal`'s content no longer stays hidden after a dimmed sheet inside it dismisses. A sheet covered by the sheets stacked over it, dimmed or not, is now hidden from TalkBack. The screen behind is reachable as soon as a sheet starts dismissing, and the grabber keeps TalkBack focus and its detent value across prop updates. ([#1063](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1063) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: A `TextInput` in a sheet now starts an autofill session when it is focused, so autofill suggestions and password managers work there. ([#1060](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1060) by [@setoy](https://github.com/setoy) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Web**: `dismiss()` before `onWillPresent` now cancels an `initialDetentIndex` present, as on iOS and Android, instead of letting the sheet slide up first. A sheet navigator route that redirects or goes back on its first render no longer flashes its sheet, unless Expo Router loads the route lazily (see Troubleshooting). ([#1055](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1055) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Web**: With server rendering (e.g. Expo Router's `web.output: 'server'`), a sheet presented with `initialDetentIndex` is now in the server's HTML, dim included, instead of sliding in once the page hydrates. Form sheets (`presentation: 'form'`) still slide in. `TrueSheetOverlay` no longer causes a hydration mismatch. ([#977](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/977) by [@simondaigre](https://github.com/simondaigre) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- `ReanimatedTrueSheet` and `ReanimatedTrueSheetScreen` no longer log "findHostInstance_DEPRECATED is deprecated in StrictMode" under React StrictMode. ([#1037](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1037) by [@setoy](https://github.com/setoy))
- `ReanimatedTrueSheet` and `ReanimatedTrueSheetScreen` no longer trigger Reanimated's "Dependencies should only be used on the web and are always ignored on native" warning on react-native-reanimated 4.6 and later. ([#1053](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1053) by [@setoy](https://github.com/setoy))
- A sheet navigator route that closes before its sheet presents, such as one that redirects or calls `goBack()` on its first render, is now removed without showing the sheet. It used to leave a grabber-height sheet over a dim and stay in the navigation state, swallowing the next back action. ([#1050](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1050) by [@maxlapides](https://github.com/maxlapides) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- With the keyboard open, an `auto` sheet with a scrollable and an absolute `footer` no longer leaves a gap as tall as the bottom inset between the content and the footer. The content also keeps its distance from the footer when `footerOptions.keyboardOffset` is set. A relative footer, or an absolute one with `avoidKeyboard: false`, no longer leaves empty space above the keyboard while the keyboard covers it. On iOS, the sheet now settles at the correct height when the keyboard opens during presentation. ([#1045](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1045), [#1057](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1057), [#1065](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1065) by [@cgrossbyte](https://github.com/cgrossbyte) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- With the keyboard open, a scrollable that ends above or partly under an absolute `footer` now scrolls its end above the footer, so a focused input near the end no longer stays hidden behind it. A footer moved by `footerOptions.keyboardOffset` or a `footerStyle` `bottom` offset is accounted for, and a footer fully behind the keyboard no longer pushes the focused input higher than needed. ([#1058](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1058) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 💥 Breaking changes

- Sheet navigator screens with `reanimated: true` now need `import '@lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/navigation/reanimated'` once, before the navigator renders (e.g. at the top of the root layout). Without it, they warn in development and run `positionChangeHandler` on the JS thread. Apps that don't import it no longer bundle `react-native-reanimated` and `react-native-worklets` with the navigator. ([#978](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/978) by [@simondaigre](https://github.com/simondaigre))

### 💡 Others

- Documented the iOS 26+ floating sheet layout: iPhone sheets float with side and bottom margins below the full detent, and `UIDesignRequiresCompatibility` is the only opt-out. Added to the troubleshooting page, the Liquid Glass guide, and the `truesheet-usage` skill. ([#1035](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1035) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Documented that keyboard handling on Android needs `adjustResize`: with `adjustPan`, focusing an input low in the sheet can push its top off-screen and leave a gap above the keyboard. Added to the Keyboard guide with a workaround for apps that need `adjustPan`, the troubleshooting page, and the `truesheet-usage` skill. ([#1046](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1046) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

## 4.1.1

### 🎉 New features

- **iOS**: Added Swift Package Manager support for apps using React Native's SwiftPM setup (`npx react-native spm`, React Native 0.88 and later). CocoaPods is unchanged. ([#1013](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1013) by [@VladyslavMartynov10](https://github.com/VladyslavMartynov10) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- **iOS**: Navigating to a modal screen while the sheet is dismissing now opens the modal once the sheet is gone. With the patched `react-native-screens`, the modal never opened and no other modal could open until the app restarted. ([#1019](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1019) by [@JacquesLeupin](https://github.com/JacquesLeupin))
- **iOS**: A sheet navigator sheet opened while the base screen shows a `fullScreenModal` now presents over the modal. It used to never appear, its `dismiss()` rejected with "No sheet found", and the half-open route kept the modal from closing through the navigator. A sheet rendered beside the navigation tree, such as a root-level named sheet, now presents over a `fullScreenModal` too. ([#1021](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1021) by [@maxlapides](https://github.com/maxlapides))
- **iOS**: The app no longer crashes with "Collection was mutated while being enumerated" when a sheet's `footer` mounts or unmounts while the keyboard is moving, such as a footer with a search input that is focused as soon as it appears. ([#1030](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1030) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: A sheet whose keyboard starts rising as the present slide ends, such as one that focuses an input around `onWillPresent`, now finishes presenting. It used to never fire `onDidPresent` or resolve `present()`, and kept swallowing taps. ([#1012](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1012) by [@JacquesLeupin](https://github.com/JacquesLeupin))
- **Android**: When a sheet presented over another sheet changes its content size right before it dismisses, the sheet behind it now slides back instead of staying pushed down and not draggable. ([#1015](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1015) by [@idlework](https://github.com/idlework))
- **Android**: The keyboard no longer counts against `maxContentHeight`, as on iOS. A sheet now rises above the keyboard instead of stopping at `maxContentHeight` with the keyboard covering the bottom of its content, such as a button below a text input. A sheet already at `maxContentHeight` now rises with the keyboard too, instead of staying put. ([#1017](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1017) by [@wildseansy](https://github.com/wildseansy))
- **Android**: A sheet whose `detents`, `maxContentHeight`, `placement`, or `maxContentWidth` change while it presents now uses the new values. It used to keep the old ones, such as settling at the old detents with empty space below its content. ([#1022](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1022) by [@Kijomimi](https://github.com/Kijomimi) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: Presenting a sheet with the keyboard up from inside a react-native-screens form sheet no longer hangs. The present used to wait for the keyboard to finish hiding on a view the form sheet stops insets animations from reaching, so it never ran and the screen ignored every tap until a back press. ([#1023](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1023) by [@jesseinkfnd](https://github.com/jesseinkfnd))
- **Android**: A sheet with `grabber={false}` whose `scrollableRef` scroll view mounts after the sheet presents, such as content that shows a loader first, now hands drags to that scroll view. A short pull with `dismissThreshold="short"` used to spring back instead of closing the sheet, and dragging down a scrolled list moved the sheet instead of scrolling the list back up. ([#1024](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1024) by [@isaacrowntree](https://github.com/isaacrowntree))
- **Android**: With three or more sheets stacked, the sheets under the top sheet now stay behind it when the app returns from the background or a prop such as `backgroundColor` changes on one of them. A sheet two or more levels under the top sheet used to come back above it. ([#1029](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1029) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 💡 Others

- The Expo example in `example/expo` now runs SDK 58, and the SDK 57 example moves to `example/expo-57` (`yarn expo-57`). The bare example is upgraded to React Native 0.88. ([#1016](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1016) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

## 4.1.0

### 🎉 New features

- Added `dimBlur` to blur the content behind the sheet instead of tinting it with `dimColor`. It takes the `backgroundBlur` styles and draws the matching iOS material's tint over the blur, so the dim looks the same on every platform. `dimOpacity` sets the blur intensity. Android blurs on Android 12 and later unless the system turns window blurs off, and only tints otherwise. ([#991](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/991) by [@VladyslavMartynov10](https://github.com/VladyslavMartynov10) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- **iOS**: Dismissing a sheet shortly after it presents over another sheet no longer hides the sheet behind it for a few frames when the top sheet's content resized right after presenting without changing any of its detent heights, such as a sheet with only fractional detents. ([#1003](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1003) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: A sheet with `backgroundColor` no longer renders transparent on iOS 27 in apps built with Xcode 26 that set `UIDesignRequiresCompatibility`. ([#1009](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1009) by [@idlework](https://github.com/idlework))
- **Android**: A sheet with `draggable={false}` no longer takes over a vertical gesture from its content, so a map inside it keeps its pinch and pan. ([#1006](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1006) by [@MarkusWendorf](https://github.com/MarkusWendorf))
- **Android**: A back press during a sheet's dismiss animation now reaches the sheet below or navigation, instead of being swallowed by the closing sheet. A sheet dismissed right after presenting without animation no longer keeps catching back presses after it closes. ([#1008](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1008) by [@wildseansy](https://github.com/wildseansy))
- A sheet with a `TrueSheetPeek` inside a `ScrollView` now settles at the full `peek` detent when dragged down after scrolling the content, instead of a thin band at the bottom of the screen. ([#1002](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1002) by [@vilindberg](https://github.com/vilindberg) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The `ReanimatedTrueSheet` Jest mock now renders `background` and `backgroundStyle` like the `TrueSheet` mock. The `TrueSheetActions` navigation mock's `pop()` now defaults to a count of 1 and `popTo` now passes `merge`, matching the real actions. ([#1005](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1005) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: A sheet that grew for the keyboard now returns to its detent when the keyboard goes away without an animation, such as after switching to another input method. It used to stay at the keyboard height. Cancelling a back gesture that was hiding the keyboard now brings the sheet back above the keyboard. ([#1007](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1007) by [@TheInkedEngineer](https://github.com/TheInkedEngineer) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 💡 Others

- Add a Dim Blur section to the Dimming guide, and the `truesheet-usage` skill now covers `dimBlur`. ([#991](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/991) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The docs and the `truesheet-usage` skill now say that iOS 27 ignores `UIDesignRequiresCompatibility` only in apps built with the iOS 27 SDK or later. ([#1009](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/1009) by [@idlework](https://github.com/idlework))

## 4.0.1

### 🐛 Bug fixes

- **iOS**: `present()` on a sheet that isn't in a window, such as on a screen that just regained focus but isn't back on screen yet, now logs a warning instead of only rejecting. Present it once the screen is visible, or use `initialDetentIndex`. ([#990](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/990) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: Closing an image picker presented over a sheet no longer leaves the sheet's content hidden from VoiceOver and UI tests. ([#994](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/994) by [@erickreutz](https://github.com/erickreutz))
- **iOS**: Closing a sheet route, `dismiss()`, or `TrueSheet.dismissAll()` while the sheet's screen is covered by a pushed screen now dismisses the sheet, instead of it coming back unclosable when the screen reappears. ([#993](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/993) by [@wildseansy](https://github.com/wildseansy) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **Android**: A fling now settles the sheet at the next detent in its direction, like iOS and Compose Material3's sheet. A fling down from the middle of three detents, or from the top of two, used to settle back unless released past halfway. ([#996](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/996) by [@philiplindberg](https://github.com/philiplindberg))
- Changing `detents` while the sheet is presented now keeps it at the same detent: adding or removing a detent below it no longer moves the sheet. If its detent is removed, the sheet moves to the detent now at the same index. `onDetentChange` reports the new index or detent. ([#999](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/999) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 💡 Others

([#984](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/984), [#985](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/985) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Add the 4.0 release blog post and a Dismissing guide for `dismissible`, `onDismissAttempt`, and `dismissThreshold`, with new header, footer, and dismissing previews. The Resizing guide is now a Detents guide covering every detent type, including `auto` with scrollables, and the guides are sorted by title. The `truesheet-usage` skill now covers the `draggable` dim-tap caveat and confirming dismissals in the sheet navigator. ([#986](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/986), [#987](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/987) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- `npx skills add lodev09/react-native-true-sheet` now offers only the `truesheet-usage` skill; the internal `changelog`, `e2e`, and `preview` skills are hidden. ([#989](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/989) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Add a Changing Detents section to the Detents guide, and the `truesheet-usage` skill now covers changing `detents` while the sheet is presented. ([#999](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/999) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

## 4.0.0

### 🎉 New features

- **iOS**: Smoother `onPositionChange`, and `onWillDismiss` now fires only when a drag-to-dismiss is committed. ([#744](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/744), [#756](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/756) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- **iOS**: Added `foldOptions` for foldables on iOS 27.1+. In tabletop, `hingeDetentIndex` rests that detent on the hinge; `hingeBehavior: 'avoid'` keeps the sheet below the hinge, and `'snap'` also moves it there when the device folds. ([#939](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/939) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- New `TrueSheetOverlay` component renders its children above every presented sheet — for toasts, dialogs, and other overlays. Replaces the `FullWindowOverlay`/`Modal` workaround. ([#817](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/817) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09) and [@SamuelBrucksch](https://github.com/SamuelBrucksch))
- Added a JS-only `lazy` prop — set to `false` to mount sheet content before presentation, allowing `auto` detents to measure settled content before the sheet opens. ([#792](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/792) by [@maxlapides](https://github.com/maxlapides))
- The `auto` detent now works with plugged scrollables — the sheet sizes to the scrollable's content height. ([#743](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/743) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- New `headerOptions` prop with a `position` option — set to `'absolute'` to float the header over the content and exclude it from the `auto` detent height. ([#747](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/747) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The footer now absorbs the bottom safe-area inset as padding when `insetAdjustment` is `automatic`, except while the keyboard is open. ([#749](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/749), [#750](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/750) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- New `scrollableOptions.keyboardOffset` option to adjust the scrollable's keyboard bottom inset. ([#785](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/785) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The dev menu's element inspector now works inside a presented sheet — header, content, and footer are all inspectable. ([#832](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/832) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- New `onDismissAttempt` event fires when the user tries to dismiss a sheet with `dismissible` set to `false` — confirm unsaved changes, then call `dismiss()`. ([#833](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/833), [#967](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/967) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Added `footerOptions.avoidKeyboard` on iOS and Android. Set it to `false` to keep absolute footers behind the keyboard while preserving space for any uncovered portion. ([#849](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/849) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Added `background` and `backgroundStyle` for custom backgrounds, exact colors, and glass or blur tints on all platforms. ([#874](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/874) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Added `detentBackgrounds` with color strings or color/blur objects, native iOS transitions, and cross-fades elsewhere. ([#876](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/876) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Added `dismissThreshold` on Android and Web. Set it to `'short'` to dismiss on a short drag or fling, like Compose Material3's sheet. ([#880](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/880) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Support Expo SDK 58 and React Native 0.88. The Expo Router `Sheet` navigator works on Router 58, and reopening a sheet on iOS no longer shows a blank screen. ([#896](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/896), [#968](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/968) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Added `dimColor` and `dimOpacity` to customize the color and opacity of the dim behind the sheet. ([#911](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/911), [#919](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/919), [#925](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/925) by [@IjzerenHein](https://github.com/IjzerenHein), [@nickcernera](https://github.com/nickcernera) and [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))

### 🐛 Bug fixes

### 💥 Breaking changes

- **iOS**: Removed `blurOptions`; use a third-party blur in `background` for custom intensity. Blur now takes precedence over `backgroundColor`; use `backgroundStyle.backgroundColor` for glass/blur tints and exact colors. ([#875](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/875) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The footer now lays out relative by default, taking up space below the content — set `footerOptions.position` to `'absolute'` to restore the previous floating behavior. ([#748](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/748), [#754](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/754) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- Content now wraps its children's height by default instead of filling the sheet — pass `flex: 1` via `style` to fill. ([#746](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/746) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The container is now sized to the sheet's visible height per detent and tracks it in realtime while dragging, requiring React Native 0.82+. ([#735](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/735), [#949](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/949) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- `anchor` is renamed to `placement` and `anchorOffset` to `placementOffset`, with `'left'` / `'right'` becoming `'leading'` / `'trailing'` to match the layout-direction behavior native already had — web now follows layout direction too. The new default `'automatic'` lets the system decide and only differs from `'center'` on iOS 27+, where placement uses the native `preferredPlacement` API. ([#846](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/846) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The scrollable's bottom inset is now applied natively on all platforms: the safe-area inset while the content can scroll (mirroring iOS's `contentInsetAdjustmentBehavior="automatic"`) and the height of an absolute footer floating over it, which then counts toward the `auto` detent — no more manual `paddingBottom`. Control it with the new `scrollableOptions.contentInsetAdjustment` (`'automatic'` | `'safe-area'` | `'footer'` | `'never'`). ([#784](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/784), [#796](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/796), [#848](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/848), [#983](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/983) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The `scrollable` prop is replaced by `scrollableRef` — pass a ref of your `ScrollView`/`FlatList` to wire nested scrolling, keyboard insets, and `auto` detent sizing. ([#795](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/795) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
- The sheet navigator is now built on the new `standard-navigation` peer dependency, with first-class Expo Router support via the `Sheet` layout from the new `/navigation/expo-router` entry point. ([#772](https://github.com/lodev09/react-native-true-sheet/pull/772) by [@lodev09](https://github.com/lodev09))
